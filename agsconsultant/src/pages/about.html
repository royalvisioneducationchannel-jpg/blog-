Who we are

A student-first consulting approach.

AGS Consultant is an education consultancy based in Faisalabad, Pakistan. We help students understand study-abroad pathways and navigate the information and steps involved in exploring an international education.

We believe that good guidance starts by listening. Every student has a different academic background, budget, preferred destination and goal. Our role is to discuss those priorities, explain relevant processes and help students identify questions to verify with universities and official authorities.

From early course research to application preparation and language learning options, we aim to make the next step easier to understand — without promising outcomes that are decided by others.
