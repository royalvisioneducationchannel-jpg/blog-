Test overview

What is LanguageCert?

LanguageCert offers English language assessments and qualifications. The right exam and format depend on why you need a result — such as an education application, professional purpose or another requirement.

Before preparing or booking, identify the exact qualification requested by the university, employer or authority you are applying to. Similar-sounding test names may have different formats, conditions or acceptance rules.

Verify the exact requirement. Acceptance, test format and required results vary by institution and purpose. Confirm current details with the organisation receiving your result and check LanguageCert’s official information before registering.

Ask on WhatsApp
Will every university accept LanguageCert?

Not necessarily. Acceptance depends on the specific institution, program, qualification and application. Confirm current requirements directly with the organisation receiving your result.

Which LanguageCert exam should I take?

Choose only after checking the exact qualification and format requested by your university, employer or authority. Contact that organisation if its instructions are unclear.

Can AGS Consultant guarantee a particular result?

No. Examination results are determined by the official assessment and your performance. AGS Consultant can discuss preparation support, but cannot guarantee a score, admission or visa outcome.

Where can I check current exam details?

Check the official LanguageCert website for current exam information and confirm acceptance and score requirements with the organisation where you plan to apply.
