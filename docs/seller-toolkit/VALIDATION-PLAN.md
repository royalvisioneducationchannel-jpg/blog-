# Seller Validation Plan (Phase 01)

_Prepared only. **Nobody is contacted** until the owner instructs. Do not offer discounts or promises in interviews._

## Five interview questions (Urdu/English, about 15 minutes)

1. **"Pichhle hafte apni product photos tayyar karne mein aap ne kya kiya? Kaun si app use ki aur kahan masla aaya?"**
   _Walk me through how you prepared product photos last week. Which apps did you use, and where did it get stuck?_
2. **"Aap ke orders kahan se aate hain (WhatsApp, Instagram, Daraz, website)? Unhein aap kahan likhte ya track karte hain?"**
   _Where do your orders come from, and where do you record or track them today?_
3. **"Courier se COD ka paisa aane ka aap kaise pata lagate hain? Aakhri dafa kis order ka paisa match nahi hua, aur kya kiya?"**
   _How do you check which COD payments the courier has actually sent? Tell me about the last mismatch._
4. **"Kisi order par asal munafa kitna hua, yeh aap kaise nikaalte hain? Kaun se kharche aksar bhool jaate hain?"**
   _How do you work out the real profit on an order? Which costs get missed?_
5. **"In mein se kis kaam ke liye aap ne kabhi paise diye hain (app, banda, software)? Kitne aur kyun?"**
   _Have you ever paid for any of these jobs (an app, a person, software)? How much, and why?_

Ask about **past behaviour**, not opinions about hypothetical features. Record exact quotes. Don't pitch.

## Pilot checklist (when instructed)

- [ ] Recruit 5–8 real sellers. Mix: WhatsApp/Instagram-only, Daraz, own store. Get consent to take notes; no recordings without permission.
- [ ] Each seller uses the **dev site** on their own phone for 3 tasks: resize for a marketplace, compress, make an invoice.
- [ ] Observe without helping. Note where they hesitate, fail or ask.
- [ ] Ask them to upload a **sample (anonymised) courier payment statement**. Collect only with consent; never real customer data in the repo.
- [ ] Record per seller: device, connection, task success (yes/no), time, quotes, willingness to pay (stated plus any past spend).
- [ ] Summarise: which hypotheses (H-A…H-E in COMPETITOR-RESEARCH.md) gained or lost support.
- [ ] Decide Seller Desk go / no-go / reshape **before** Phase 08 build effort.
- [ ] Delete personal contact data after the pilot unless the seller opts in to follow-up.
