Quick answers to questions students often ask before contacting us.

View all FAQs
Is the first counselling session free?

Yes. Your first counselling session with AGS Consultant is free. We discuss your goals, academic background and options before suggesting next steps.

Where can I get help with my admission application?

Visit AGS Consultant at Office No. 108–111, Center Point Plaza, Kohinoor City, Jaranwala Road, Faisalabad, or reach us by phone and email. Our team can discuss your study goals and explain the available application-support process.

Can AGS Consultant help me prepare for an English test?

AGS Consultant lists preparation options for IELTS, LanguageCert, Duolingo English Test, spoken English and German language. Contact us to confirm current courses, schedules and availability.

Can you guarantee admission, a visa or a job?

No. Admissions and visa decisions are made by universities and relevant authorities, and employment outcomes cannot be guaranteed. We provide guidance and application support, but every case depends on its own eligibility and official decisions.
