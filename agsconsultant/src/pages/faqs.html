Study abroad & admissions

What qualifications do I need to study in the UK?

Entry requirements vary by institution and course. Students with intermediate qualifications may be eligible for undergraduate pathways, while graduates can explore postgraduate options. Your academic background, English-language ability and chosen course all affect eligibility. Contact us for advice based on your profile.

When do courses in the UK begin?

Start dates differ between universities, colleges and programs. Many institutions have multiple intakes during the year, but availability varies. Check your chosen course dates and apply early so there is time for admissions and visa steps.

English tests & language

Can AGS Consultant help me prepare for an English test?

AGS Consultant lists preparation options for IELTS, LanguageCert, Duolingo English Test, spoken English and German language. Contact us to confirm current courses, schedules and availability.

Should I take IELTS Academic or General Training?

It depends on your purpose. Academic is commonly used for higher education and some professional registration; General Training may be used for certain migration or non-academic purposes. Confirm the exact test with the university, employer or authority receiving your result.

Do all universities accept the Duolingo English Test?

No. Acceptance depends on the institution, program and application circumstances. Check with the specific admissions office for current requirements before booking.

Can you guarantee admission, a visa or a job?

No. Admissions and visa decisions are made by universities and relevant authorities, and employment outcomes cannot be guaranteed. We can provide guidance and application support, but every case depends on its own eligibility and official decisions.
