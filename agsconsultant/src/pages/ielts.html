About the test

What is IELTS?

IELTS (International English Language Testing System) is an English-language proficiency test accepted by many universities, employers and organisations. It assesses how effectively you can use English in listening, reading, writing and speaking.

Important: Test type, score requirements and accepted versions depend on the institution or authority you are applying to. Always confirm the exact requirement with them before booking a test.

Test structure

Four skills. One complete picture.

The test measures different parts of your English ability. The exact timing and delivery options can depend on the test format and location — check official IELTS information when booking.

01

Listening

Understand main ideas, details, opinions and purpose in spoken English.

02

Reading

Read for main ideas, specific information, argument and writer’s purpose.

03

Writing

Organise ideas clearly, respond to tasks and use appropriate vocabulary and grammar.

04

Speaking

Communicate ideas and experiences clearly in a structured conversation.

Understanding your result

IELTS band scores.

IELTS reports a band score for each skill and an overall band score. The summary below is a general guide, not an admission guarantee.

9Expert user
8Very good user
7Good user
6Competent user
5Modest user
4Limited user
3Extremely limited
0–2Very limited / no attempt

Band descriptions are simplified. Use official IELTS guidance for precise score interpretation and verify the score you need with your receiving organisation.

Should I take IELTS Academic or General Training?

It depends on your purpose. Academic is commonly used for higher education and some professional registration; General Training may be used for certain migration or non-academic purposes. Confirm the exact test with the university, employer or authority receiving your result.

What IELTS score will I need?

There is no single score requirement for every course or application. Requirements can include an overall band and minimum scores for individual skills. Check the current official requirements for your chosen course or pathway.

How should I start preparing?

Start by confirming your test type and target score. Then assess your current level, learn the test format, practise each skill regularly and review your errors. Use reliable, current practice resources.

Does AGS Consultant guarantee a particular IELTS score?

No. Test scores are determined by the official test process and an individual’s performance. AGS Consultant can discuss available preparation support, but cannot guarantee a score, admission or visa outcome.
