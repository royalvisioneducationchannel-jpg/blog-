Royal Vision Education ki Android app

Ye app ka chalta hua namoona hai. Phone mein har button dabayein: eligibility check karein, mulk dekhein, appointment book karein. Design aap ki website ke rang aur logo se liya gaya hai.

Abhi koi data kahin nahi jata. Asal app mein forms seedha counselor ke WhatsApp aur admin panel tak jayein ge.

Is demo mein kya hai

  • Home · trust stats, quick actions, IELTS offer, website ke latest blogs.
  • Check · 5 sawal, score /100, munasib mulk, WhatsApp par counselor ko bhejein.
  • Countries · har mulk ka page, intake aur zaroori documents.
  • Book · 5 branches, din aur waqt chunein, confirmation.
  • My File · Phase 2 ka preview: file ka status, documents ki checklist.
  • AlertsUpar ghanti ka nishan · deadlines aur events ke push notifications.
