Royal Vision Education is a study abroad consultancy in Pakistan, working since 2009 with branches at the Head Office, Faisalabad, Lahore, Islamabad and Hafizabad. The brand is a deep royal plum on clean white, set in heavy Poppins, and it should feel trusted, clear and quick to act on.

## Logo

- Use `royal-vision-education-logo.png` (full lockup) in headers, documents and social posts. Use `royal-vision-education-mark.png` (graduate in the ring) only where the space is square: app icon, profile picture, favicon, watermark.
- Place the logo on `surface` or `rv-blush`. On `rv-brand` or photos, put it on a white panel; never recolour it.
- Keep clear space around it equal to the height of the SINCE 2009 bar. Minimum width: 140px for the lockup, 32px for the mark.
- Do not stretch, outline, add shadows or retype the wordmark.

## Colour

- Lead with `rv-brand` (#9E0952). It is the colour people know the brand by: primary buttons, links, counters and icons.
- Use `rv-main` (#D6005F) as the bright accent: one highlighted word in a heading, hover glow, the end of the brand gradient.
- The brand gradient is `rv-light` → `rv-main` at 135deg. Use it on one hero button or badge per screen, not on whole sections.
- Text: headings in `rv-dark`, body in `rv-text`, stronger secondary text in `rv-slate`. The home hero title uses `rv-hero-ink`.
- Backgrounds: `surface` for cards, `rv-blush` for alternating sections (a 180deg fade from `surface` to `rv-blush`), `rv-blush-2` for chips and icon wells. Borders and hairlines are `rv-tint`.
- `rv-green` + `wa-dark` belong to WhatsApp buttons only; `yt-red` to YouTube only. Do not use them as brand colours.
- Never put small text in `rv-light` on white (3.9:1). Never put white text on plain `rv-green` (2.0:1); use the WhatsApp gradient with a bold label.

## Type

- Set everything in Poppins (`sans`). Inter (`ui`) appears only in a few form widgets; prefer Poppins for new work.
- Headings are heavy: `hero-title` 800 with -2px tracking (scale it down to 42px on phones), `section-title` 800 with -1px, `heading` 600.
- Body copy is `body` (15px, 1.7) or `body-sm` (14px, 1.65) in `rv-text`.
- Section labels above headings use `eyebrow`: 12px, weight 800, 2px tracking, uppercase, in `rv-brand`.
- Numbers in stats (100+, Since 2009) use Poppins 800 in `rv-brand`.

## Shape, space and depth

- Every button, badge and chip is a pill (`radius-pill`). Cards use `radius-lg`; large panels `radius-xl`; inputs `radius-md`.
- Space with `space-12` inside cards, `space-18` between cards and as card padding, `space-48` for section padding.
- Cards rest on `shadow-card` with an `rv-tint` border and lift to `shadow-raised` on hover. Backgrounds may carry the faint `rv-grid` graph-paper pattern (1px lines).

## Voice

- Speak to Pakistani students and parents in clear English, with a warm local opening where it fits: "Assalam o Alaikum 👋".
- Lead with the action and keep it short: "Get Free Assessment", "Start Your Journey", "Book Now", "Explore UK →".
- Promise guidance, not outcomes. Say "maximize your visa chances", "expert guidance", "free consultation". Any number (visa success, students guided) must be backed by real records.
- Name real things: the intake ("UK January 2027 intake"), the test ("IELTS / PTE"), the branch ("Lahore branch").
- Contact details appear as written: +92 334 5298703, qamar@royalvision.com.pk, office hours Monday–Friday, 10am–6pm.

## Iconography

- Countries are shown with their flag emoji (🇬🇧 🇺🇸 🇦🇺 🇨🇾 🇯🇵 🇭🇺 🇨🇦) next to the country name.
- The six-step student journey uses one emoji per step (💬 🏛️ 📄 🚀 🎤 ✅). Keep that set and order.
- Elsewhere the site uses Font Awesome line icons in `rv-brand` on `rv-blush-2` wells.
