Helpful habits for language learning.

Regular practice between lessons can help you keep new language active.

  1. 1

    Practise a little, often

    Short, regular practice sessions can make it easier to revisit vocabulary and grammar.

  2. 2

    Use German aloud

    Repeat phrases, read short passages aloud and practise conversations whenever possible.

  3. 3

    Review and ask questions

    Keep notes on new words and ask your instructor for clarification when something is unclear.

Do I need previous German experience to start?

Many learners begin with little or no prior knowledge, but class placement depends on the course structure. Ask AGS Consultant whether a beginner level or placement assessment is available.

What is the course schedule and fee?

Schedules, fees, class format and availability can change. Please contact AGS Consultant directly for the latest information before making plans.

Will the course provide an official language certificate?

A course completion certificate and an externally recognised language qualification are not necessarily the same. Confirm with AGS which, if any, certificate is included and whether it meets the requirements of your intended institution or authority.

Can German lessons guarantee admission, a visa or a job?

No. Language learning can be one part of preparation, but admission, visa and employment decisions depend on the relevant institutions, authorities and employers. Check their current requirements directly.
