Course overview

Turn what you know into everyday conversation.

Many learners study English at school and become comfortable reading or writing, but still want more opportunities to speak. A spoken-English course can offer structured practice in expressing ideas, listening to others and taking part in common conversations.

Lessons may focus on useful everyday language, speaking clearly and communicating appropriately in different situations. Course content, class format and level placement depend on the current program offered by AGS Consultant.

Ask us for current details. Confirm class levels, schedule, fees, format and course availability directly with AGS Consultant before enrolling.

Getting started

How to make the most of your learning.

Progress takes time. A supportive routine can help you keep practising between lessons.

  1. 1

    Choose a realistic goal

    Think about where you want to use English and which situations feel most difficult today.

  2. 2

    Practise out loud

    Use short daily exercises, repeat useful phrases and look for chances to have simple conversations.

  3. 3

    Review and keep going

    Notice what is improving, ask questions and keep practising without worrying about every mistake.

Is this course suitable if I understand English but hesitate to speak?

The course is designed for people who want more speaking practice. Contact AGS Consultant to discuss your current level and whether the available class is suitable for your goals.

What are the course timings and fees?

Class schedules, fees, format and availability may change. Please contact AGS Consultant directly for the latest details.

Will the course guarantee fluency?

No course can guarantee a particular level of fluency. Progress depends on your starting point, practice, attendance and individual learning pace. Ask the team what learning outcomes the current course is designed to support.

Does this spoken-English course prepare me for IELTS?

General conversation practice and IELTS test preparation have different aims. If you need IELTS preparation, ask AGS Consultant about the IELTS-specific course and check your institution’s score requirements.
