Test overview

What is the Duolingo English Test?

The Duolingo English Test (DET) is an English proficiency test that some institutions accept as evidence of English ability. It is delivered online, but acceptance, required results and conditions are set by each university or organisation.

Before choosing a test, confirm that your specific institution accepts the DET for your course, intake and application type. Check whether it sets a minimum overall result, section-level requirements or additional conditions.

Always check official requirements first. Acceptance and test conditions may vary and can change. Verify the latest information with your chosen institution and the official Duolingo English Test website before registering or paying.

English skills

Prepare across the language skills.

Effective preparation can include practice in the skills used to communicate in English. Use official, current preparation materials to understand the test format.

01

Reading

Practise understanding written English, vocabulary in context and the main ideas in short texts.

02

Listening

Build focus when following spoken English and identifying meaning from what you hear.

03

Writing

Practise organising ideas and expressing them clearly using accurate written English.

04

Speaking

Work on responding to prompts clearly, speaking naturally and communicating your ideas.

Frequently asked questions

Duolingo English Test FAQs.

General information to help you plan your next step.

Ask on WhatsApp
Do all universities accept the Duolingo English Test?

No. Acceptance depends on the institution, program and application circumstances. Check with the specific admissions office for current requirements before booking.

What result will I need?

There is no single result requirement for every school or course. Confirm the current minimum result and any additional conditions with the institution where you plan to apply.

Can AGS Consultant guarantee a particular test result?

No. Test results depend on the official assessment and your performance. AGS Consultant can discuss preparation guidance, but cannot guarantee a score, admission or visa outcome.

Where should I check the test's latest rules?

Use the official Duolingo English Test website for current registration, identification, equipment, environment, test rules and result information. Also verify your institution’s own requirements.
