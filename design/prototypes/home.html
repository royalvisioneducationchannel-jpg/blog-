Questions

Are my photos uploaded?+

No. Resize, compress, square, bulk and watermark run on your device. Only the background remover (when available) sends the photo you choose to a removal service, and it says so before you click.

Do I need an account?+

Not for free tools. An account will be needed for saved presets and paid plans.
